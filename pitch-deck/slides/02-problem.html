The problem

Conference badges are how many developers first touch hardware.

Closed

Most badges can't be changed or rebuilt by the people who wear them.

Single-event

Made for one conference, then put away.

Imported

Hardware kits are often imported, expensive and single-purpose.

Students rarely get a complete device (screen, input, sensors, radio, battery) that they can program in a beginner-friendly language and keep using.
