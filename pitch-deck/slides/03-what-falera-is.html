What Falera is

One badge. One open OS.

The Falera Badge

An ESP32-S3 badge with a 2.8″ touch screen, eight buttons, IR, a motion sensor, a speaker and microSD, on a board anyone can build.

FaleraOS

A MicroPython operating system with a home screen, apps, an on-device App Store and over-the-air updates.
