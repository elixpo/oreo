# Music

Your own music on the PCB Falera Badge's speaker.

**microSD card.** Copy MP3 files onto a microSD card with a computer, in
any folders, and put it in the badge. Browse folders, play a folder (first
row) or a song, or play the whole card. Shuffle and Repeat (off, all, one)
are on the home screen. Title, artist and album come from the files' ID3
tags.

Music plays only the MP3s on the card; it doesn't connect to anything.

On the player: **LEFT/RIGHT** previous / next, **UP/DOWN** volume, **A**
play/stop, **B** back. Touch works everywhere.
