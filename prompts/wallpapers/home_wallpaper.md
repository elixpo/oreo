# home_wallpaper — OreoOS home screen wallpaper (light, liquid glass)

**Asset name:** `home_wallpaper`
**Source size:** landscape 1536×1024 (3:2) → cropped and optimized to 320×240
**Used in:** the home screen behind the clock, date and status bar (`oreoOS/home.py`)
**Theme reference:** See [../icons/THEME.md](../icons/THEME.md)

The home screen draws dark text (rgb 38,38,48) on top, so the image must stay light and calm, with the most detail away from the centre-left where the clock sits. Original art in the spirit of a modern phone wallpaper, not a copy of any existing one.
