# splash_wallpaper — OreoOS boot splash wallpaper (dark)

**Asset name:** `splash_bg` (replaces `assets/sprites/raw/splash_bg.jpg`)
**Source size:** landscape 1536×1024 (3:2) → cropped to 320×240, dimmed in `tools/build_splash.py`
**Used in:** the boot splash behind the rolling "hello" and the loader (`oreoOS/splash.py`)
**Theme reference:** See [../icons/THEME.md](../icons/THEME.md)

The splash draws light cream text in a band across the middle and a gold loader near the bottom, so the image must be dark with a quiet middle. Original art in the spirit of a modern phone wallpaper, not a copy of any existing one.
